ShopInsights Personal

ShopInsights Personal is a private, read-only analytics tool used by one Etsy seller to review publicly available listing data and improve their own listings. It is not available to the public and does not resell or share data.

Terms of Service

This tool is for personal use only. It does not replace or circumvent Etsy's checkout, does not post to Etsy on behalf of others, and does not sell or share Etsy data.

Privacy Policy

This tool does not collect or store personal information about Etsy members or buyers. It only reads publicly available listing metrics (such as review counts and favorites) and links back to the original listings on Etsy.
